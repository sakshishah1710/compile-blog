<h2>What changed</h2><p>GitHub Copilot now includes Gemini 4 Hydrogen as a generally available model option. This is Google's newest Flash-class model, positioned as an alternative to existing Copilot backends for users who want to try a different approach to code generation and assistance.</p><h2>The optimization focus</h2><p>Gemini 4 Hydrogen is designed specifically for speed and efficiency in agentic coding scenarios—workflows where the model chains multiple steps together or uses tools to accomplish tasks. Google emphasizes improved instruction following, which matters when you're asking the assistant to perform multi-step refactors or coordinate across files.</p><h2>What to consider</h2><p>Model choice in Copilot is becoming table stakes: you can now pick between OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google backends depending on your workflow. If you're doing complex, multi-file changes or relying heavily on Copilot's workspace features, trying Hydrogen makes sense to see if its agentic optimizations fit your patterns. For straightforward autocomplete, the differences will be less pronounced.</p>

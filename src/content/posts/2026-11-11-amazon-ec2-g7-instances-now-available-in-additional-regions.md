<h2>What's new</h2><p>AWS has expanded availability of EC2 G7 instances to four additional regions: Europe (London), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), Asia Pacific (Sydney), and South America (São Paulo). These instances run NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, positioning them for graphics rendering and AI inference workloads that were previously limited to initial launch regions.</p><h2>Performance claims</h2><p>AWS states G7 instances deliver up to 4.6x better AI inference performance and up to 2.1x better graphics performance compared to the previous-generation G6 instances. These benchmarks matter if you're running GPU-accelerated workloads like 3D rendering, virtual workstations, or edge AI inference—but note these are maximum gains, not guaranteed across all workload types.</p><h2>What this means for your deployments</h2><p>If you've been running G6 instances or shipping graphics workloads to US regions due to instance availability, you now have regional options that may reduce latency and data transfer costs. The Blackwell GPU architecture brings newer capabilities, but migration planning should include testing your specific workloads against the performance claims. G7 pricing details and per-region availability will determine whether a move makes economic sense for existing deployments.</p>

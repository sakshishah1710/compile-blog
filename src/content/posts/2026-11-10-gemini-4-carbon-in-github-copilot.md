<h2>What changed</h2><p>GitHub Copilot now includes Gemini 4 Carbon, Google's newest model, as a generally available option. The model specifically targets complex coding scenarios, agentic workflows, and reasoning-heavy tasks. Google positions it as security-focused, emphasizing secure code generation alongside its technical capabilities.</p><h2>Why it matters</h2><p>This continues GitHub's multi-model strategy, giving developers more choice in their AI coding assistant. For teams dealing with intricate codebases or security-sensitive work, having a model explicitly tuned for those use cases could mean fewer vulnerabilities introduced during AI-assisted development. That said, no model eliminates the need for code review—security claims should be validated in your context, not taken as gospel.</p><h2>What to do</h2><p>If you're already using Copilot, Gemini 4 Carbon is available now through your existing subscription. Test it on your most complex tasks—refactoring legacy code, implementing nuanced business logic, or generating infrastructure configurations. Compare its output quality and security posture against the other models you're using. The multi-model approach means you can match the tool to the task rather than forcing one model to handle everything.</p>

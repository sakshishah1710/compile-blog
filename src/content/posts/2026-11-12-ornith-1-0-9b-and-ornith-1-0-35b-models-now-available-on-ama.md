<h2>What's new</h2><p>AWS added DeepReinforce AI's Ornith-1.0-9B and Ornith-1.0-35B to SageMaker JumpStart. Both models are open-source under MIT license and designed for agentic coding tasks with self-scaffolding capabilities. They're positioned as state-of-the-art performers among comparable-sized open models in their family.</p><h2>What this means for deployment</h2><p>SageMaker JumpStart provides pre-configured deployment for these models, removing the infrastructure setup step. You get the models running on SageMaker endpoints without building your own serving infrastructure. The MIT license means you can use these commercially without the restrictions of some other open models.</p><h2>The practical angle</h2><p>If you're evaluating coding assistants or agentic workflows, these models give you another benchmark point against existing options like CodeLlama or StarCoder. The self-scaffolding aspect suggests they can break down coding tasks autonomously, though you'll need to test whether that matches your specific use cases. Having two size options (9B and 35B parameters) lets you trade off between inference cost and capability.</p>

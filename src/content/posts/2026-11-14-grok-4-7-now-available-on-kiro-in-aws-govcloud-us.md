<h2>What Changed</h2><p>AWS has made Grok 4.7 available in the Kiro IDE and CLI tools within AWS GovCloud (US) regions. Grok 4.7 is SpaceXSI's latest model designed specifically for extended coding sessions and knowledge work, with improved error recovery and planning capabilities compared to the previous 4.6 version.</p><h2>Why It Matters for GovCloud Users</h2><p>GovCloud availability means developers working under FedRAMP, ITAR, or other compliance requirements can now use this model without leaving their certified environment. The model's focus on repository-scale work and self-checking before proceeding aligns with the kind of deliberate, auditable processes government and regulated projects require. This isn't a general-purpose chatbot—it's positioned for sustained development tasks that span hours.</p><h2>What to Consider</h2><p>If you're already using Kiro in GovCloud, Grok 4.7 is worth testing on complex refactoring or multi-file changes where context needs to persist. The improved error recovery should reduce the manual intervention these tasks typically require. Standard GovCloud constraints still apply—verify your specific compliance requirements allow AI-assisted tooling before committing to production workflows.</p>

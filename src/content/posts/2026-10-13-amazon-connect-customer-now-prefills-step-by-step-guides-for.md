<h2>What changed</h2><p>Amazon Connect now automatically extracts information from customer conversations—like booking IDs, claim numbers, and call summaries—and prefills the step-by-step guides agents complete after each contact. Managers configure which extracted fields map to which form fields. Agents review and submit instead of typing everything manually.</p><h2>Why it matters</h2><p>After-contact work (ACW) is dead time in contact centers. Agents spend minutes per call documenting what just happened while the next caller waits. This cuts that overhead without changing the validation step—agents still review before submission, so you're not blindly trusting AI output. The win is speed and consistency, not elimination of human judgment.</p><h2>What to actually do</h2><p>If you run Amazon Connect, this is worth testing on high-volume queues first. Map a few common fields—customer ID, issue category, resolution notes—and measure ACW time before and after. Watch for extraction accuracy on your specific call types. The feature won't replace custom workflows, but it should reduce repetitive data entry where call structure is predictable.</p>

<h2>What changed</h2><p>AWS Agent Registry now supports custom metadata on registry records. Instead of just a name and description, you can add your own structured fields to catalog entries for agents, MCP servers, agent skills, gateways, and custom resources. This applies across the entire registry within your organization.</p><h2>Why it matters at scale</h2><p>When you're managing hundreds of agent-related resources, basic name-description pairs break down quickly. Custom metadata lets you implement organizational conventions—cost center codes, compliance classifications, team ownership, environment tags, or whatever taxonomy your governance model requires. It's about making the registry searchable and governable in ways that match how your organization actually works.</p><h2>Practical takeaway</h2><p>If you're using AWS Agent Registry and hitting friction around discovery or governance, this gives you the extension points to solve it. Define the metadata schema that fits your org, apply it consistently, and suddenly your catalog becomes a useful filter instead of a wall of entries. Start by identifying what fields would make your most common searches trivial—that's your schema.</p>
